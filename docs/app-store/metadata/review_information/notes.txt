Pairline is the iPhone half of a two-phone setup. It relays SMS, calls, contacts, call history and USSD codes from the user's own Android phone, which runs our free "Pairline Bridge" Android app, over an encrypted Bluetooth link between the two phones. There are no Pairline servers and no accounts.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT AN ANDROID PHONE
1. Launch the app and tap Continue until "How would you like to start?".
2. Choose "Explore the demo". Demo mode is free and shows every screen with sample data.
3. Tap "Try it" in the demo banner to receive a sample one-time code, a message or an incoming call.

IN-APP PURCHASES (both non-consumable)
- "7-day Trial" (free): starts a 7-day trial of all features. It needs no payment method.
- "Pairline Full Access": unlocks Pairline permanently with one payment.
Restore Purchases is on the start screen, on the paywall and in Settings. Nothing renews or is charged automatically.

CALLKIT
CallKit is used only to show calls that ring on the user's own Android phone (the SIM is in that phone). Call audio stays on the Android phone in this version. The app is not offered in China mainland.

BLUETOOTH
The "bluetooth-central" background mode keeps the link to the user's own Android phone, so messages and calls reach the iPhone while Pairline is in the background.
